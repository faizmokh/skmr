# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers managing directory-based agent skills for Codex, OpenCode, and Pi from a terminal. They need to inspect discovery state, understand ownership and scope, and make filesystem changes with confidence.

## Product Purpose

skmr provides one local library and one consistent CLI/TUI workflow for discovering, adopting, enabling, disabling, and restoring agent skills. Success means users can understand the current state quickly and apply deliberate changes without losing content or ownership information.

## Brand Personality

Pragmatic, compact, trustworthy. The interaction should feel familiar to users of LazyGit: keyboard-first, information-dense, and calm under routine use.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental dashboards, oversized headings, excessive panels, decorative color, and unfamiliar controls that slow terminal workflows. Avoid interfaces that rely on color alone to communicate state.

## Design Principles

- Make current scope, selection, and managed state obvious at a glance.
- Keep frequent actions close to the information they affect.
- Use familiar terminal navigation and visible shortcut cues.
- Give destructive or structural changes a clear, reviewable preview.
- Preserve useful density as the terminal becomes smaller.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support full keyboard operation, narrow and resized terminals, and textual state labels. Sanitize untrusted terminal content and never rely on color alone for meaning.
